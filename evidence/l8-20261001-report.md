## Scope

Only Linux inherited-lifecycle-lease reconciliation is newly authorized. The bounded audit must contain exactly nine unique string fields, the existing format/reason, matching job/token/version, `unknown`, `apply-returned-success`, and phase `version`, `history`, `artifact-retention` or `final-marker`. Active identity, available lifecycle lease and strict absence of health/pending/in-progress remain mandatory for this route. Invalid/conflicting finals are not overwritten. Existing matched-health recovery remains separate.

Recovery reuses version/history/final persistence, then updates the audit and terminal journal. It never invokes apply/rollback, invents health or deletes historical intent/locks. An interrupted sync recovery retains its original qualifying audit until the committed audit replaces it; a terminal journal error after confirmed final/audit leaves a readable final.
